Features

Everything you need to plan people, and nothing you don't

One timeline for your whole team, with the details that make a schedule trustworthy.

Drag-and-drop timeline

Draw a booking across the days you need. Drag it to another person, stretch it, or hold Alt to copy it.

Capacity at a glance

Every day shows how booked each person is. You see overbooking in red before it becomes a problem.

Holidays by state

Public holidays for each state and territory, plus shutdowns. They come out of capacity without stopping anyone from working.

Projects, phases & budgets

Give projects dates, budgeted hours and phases. Baseline flags bookings that go over budget or outside the dates.

Group by discipline or project

See the team by discipline, or switch to projects to see exactly who's on each one. Move work between projects with a drag.

Reports & exports

Capacity, billable and non-billable hours, time off and utilization by person and project. Export to CSV or JSON.

For big teams

Lots of people. Lots of projects. Still one clear picture.

As work moves between people, Baseline keeps everyone's load in view. The rings show how booked each person is, turning red when someone is over capacity, so you can rebalance before it becomes a problem.

  • Utilization for every person, this week and the next four
  • Free hours shown before you book anyone
  • Undo and redo for every change
