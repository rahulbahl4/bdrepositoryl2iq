Getting started
New to Business Development?

Here are the five things to do before you start working on opportunities. Run through these in order and you'll be set up and ready to go.

STEP 01
📖
Read the BD Process
Understand how opportunities move from identification through to submission.
STEP 02
✅
Learn the Go / No Go
Every opportunity needs a Go/No Go assessment before we commit.
STEP 03
🔑
Get Portal Access
Request access to Buy NSW, Ariba, Zycus and Coupa from the primary account holders.
STEP 04
🔔
Set Tender Alerts
Register on Australian Tenders and Vendor Panel and set up your own alerts.
STEP 05
📄
Download Cap Statements
Read our capability statements so you know which version to use for each opportunity.

Questions? Your first stop is Head of Strategy & Growth.
