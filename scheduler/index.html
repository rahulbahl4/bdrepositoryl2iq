What it does

Scheduling that actually keeps up.

  1. 01

    Drag. Drop. Done.

    Draw a booking across the days you need. Throw engineers between jobs, stretch a booking when FAT slips, or hold Alt to copy it.

  2. 02

    See the crunch coming.

    Capacity vs demand, discipline by discipline, week by week. Know you're short on Controls in March while it's still November.

  3. 03

    Model the win before it lands.

    Put bids in the pipeline with a win chance, fill unfilled slots, and flip them to confirmed the moment the PO arrives.

  4. 04

    Leave that doesn't break SAT.

    Add time off in bulk, or paste it straight from Excel. Baseline flags the work it hits and hands it over or pushes it back.

  5. 05

    Every state, every holiday.

    Public holidays for every state and territory, RDOs and shutdowns, all taken out of capacity automatically.

  6. 06

    Numbers that add up.

    Utilization, billable hours, budgets against actual bookings, and leave by type. Export everything to CSV.
