The OT project lifecycle

From design to handover, the right people at every phase

Give each job its phases and dates, then see who's needed for each one, from the first drawings to final SAT sign-off.

  1. Design

    Functional design spec, control philosophy, electrical drawings and network architecture.

    Controls · Electrical

  2. Integration

    Panel build, PLC and SCADA programming, HMI screens and network configuration.

    PLC · SCADA · Panel shop

  3. FAT

    Factory Acceptance Test: prove the system with the client at your facility before it ships.

    Controls · SCADA · PM

  4. IFAT

    Integrated FAT: test alongside other vendors' packages and the client's systems.

    Controls · Network · Vendors

  5. Install & commission

    Installation, loop checks, I/O testing, then cold and hot commissioning on site.

    Commissioning · Electrical

  6. SAT

    Site Acceptance Test (and SIT where needed): prove it on the plant and get client sign-off.

    Commissioning · Controls · PM

  7. Handover

    As-built drawings, O&M manuals, operator training and the move to support.

    PM · Support
Features

Built for engineering teams that work across sites

One timeline for every engineer, with the details that make a schedule trustworthy.

Drag-and-drop timeline

Draw a booking across the days you need. Drag engineers between jobs, stretch a booking when FAT slips, or hold Alt to copy it.

Capacity at a glance

Every day shows how booked each engineer is, so you can see when your key people are overloaded before commissioning week.

Holidays by state

Public holidays for every state and territory, plus office shutdowns. They come out of capacity, and you can still book site work on them.

Phases from design to handover

Give each job dates, budgeted hours and phases such as Design, Integration, FAT, IFAT, SAT and Commissioning. Baseline flags bookings that go over budget or outside the dates.

Group by discipline or job

Controls, SCADA, electrical, commissioning: see the team by discipline, or by job to see exactly who's on each one.

Reports & exports

Capacity, billable and non-billable hours, time off and utilization by engineer and job. Export to CSV or JSON.

For big teams

Lots of engineers. Lots of sites. Still one clear picture.

As engineers move between jobs and sites, Baseline keeps everyone's load in view. The rings show how booked each person is, turning red when someone is over capacity, so you can rebalance before it becomes a problem.

  • Utilization for every engineer, this week and the next four
  • Free hours shown before you book anyone
  • Undo and redo for every change
